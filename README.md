# AI-assisted-personal-medication-health-companion
AI-powered personal health monitor that digitizes prescriptions, sets medicine reminders, stores health records, tracks wellness, and provides emergency SOS support
1. Dashboard — your health at a glance
See your health score, key vitals, today's medicines, water intake, recent records and your emergency card on one screen.

2. My Health
Keeps your health profile in one place: medical conditions, allergies and vitals stats.

3. Medical History
A timeline of your past health events. You can filter it to find things quickly.

4. Prescription Reader (AI-assisted)
Upload a photo of a prescription. The app reads it (OCR) and shows the original next to the AI-extracted details (medicine, dose, how often, how long). It then writes a simple one-line explanation, like "Take Medicine X 500 mg, twice daily, for 5 days." The patient is asked to confirm anything the AI extracted.

5. Document Vault
Store prescriptions, lab reports, imaging and discharge summaries together. Filter by type. Each file is clearly labelled as an original record or AI-extracted.

6. Medicine Reminders
Create, edit and confirm reminders. The app turns text like "twice daily" or "at night" into real reminder times and counts the number of days to take it.

7. Wearable Data
Connect a smartwatch, Google Fit / Health Connect or Apple Health (demo mode) and see trend charts for things like heart rate, steps and sleep.

8. Hydration Tracker
Add water with one tap, watch your daily progress ring fill up, and turn on drink-water reminders.

9. Health Insights
A weekly, AI-generated summary of your wellness trends (activity, sleep, hydration, medicines). It always says: "not a medical diagnosis."
